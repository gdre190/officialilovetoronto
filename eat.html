The restaurants Toronto can't stop talking about

Closings, openings, patio launches, and the spots that matter right now

Closing · May 2026

Basil Box Is Dead. All Locations, Gone by May 14.

The GTA-born build-your-own bowl chain that peaked at 17 locations shut every restaurant this month. Founder Peter Chiu cited 'deeply personal reasons.' No sale. No restructuring. Just gone after 11 years.
Closings & Goodbyes
RIP · Michelin

Yukashi Closed for Good — Toronto Lost a Michelin Star

Chef Daisuke Izutsu's 10-seat Mount Pleasant omakase earned one of Canada's first Michelin stars in 2022. Final dinner was May 2. 'Something new is coming,' they say.

RIP · College St

Tokyo Hot Fried Chicken Closed After 7 Years on College

The Nashville-meets-karaage shop at 656 College was a neighbourhood pillar. Lease ended, owners burned out. They'll return as pop-ups — 'back to the streets where it all began.'

Plot Twist · Ossington

Bonito's Lasted Less Than a Year — Already Replaced by Bambu

The Korean-Ecuadorean retro hit at 180 Ossington closed after a liquor license mishap. Bar Bowie's team pivoted the space to 'Bambu' — cocktail-forward late-night concept. Opened May 13.

RIP · Kensington

Uncle Pete's Bread Co. Lost Its Lease on Baldwin Street

The tiny focaccia sandwich counter — OG mortadella with whipped lemon ricotta — couldn't find a new home in time. Last day was April 25.

New · Ossington

Bambu Just Opened at 180 Ossington — Cocktails, Small Bites, Late Nights

Bar Bowie's little sister concept. 'A new bar, shaped by places, flavours, music, and nights we kept thinking about long after they've ended.'

New Menu

Aloette Just Dropped a Summer Tasting Menu and It's Unhinged

Patrick Kriss goes full Mediterranean chaos — anchovies, peaches, and a dessert that takes 45 minutes.

Patio Season Is Here
CaféTO · Victoria Day

CaféTO Launched This Weekend — 290+ Restaurants Added Curb Lane Patios

The city's patio expansion program is back for 2026. Over 1,600 total outdoor dining spaces across Toronto. Ossington, Dundas West, Queen West, and the Danforth are loaded.

Paradise Grapevine: Tinned Fish + Natural Wine on Dundas West

$18 tins with natural wine pairings. There's never a wait before 6pm. One of the best low-key summer hangs in the city.
