Local Gems

The spots only residents know — no SEO lists, no influencer tags

Dundas West

Paradise Grapevine — Tinned Fish + Natural Wine, No Pretension

$18 tins with wine pairings. No wait before 6pm. One of the best low-key summer hangs in the city. The kind of place you tell three friends about.

Scarborough

Scarborough's Best Curry House Doesn't Have a Sign

A strip mall near Markham & Lawrence. Best goat roti in the GTA. Cash only. No Instagram. Your uncle probably already knows about it.

Kensington

Moonbean Coffee: The Kensington Café That Never Changed (Thank God)

While everything around it gentrifies, Moonbean stays weird, warm, and perfect. The courtyard in summer is one of the best hidden spots in the city.

East York

The Danforth Bookshop Everyone Walks Past but Should Stop At

Type Books on Queen is famous. But the used bookshop on the Danforth with the cat in the window? That's where the real finds are.

Little Italy

The $4 Espresso That Tastes Better Than Your $7 Latte

A tiny Italian café on College between Bathurst and Ossington. No oat milk. No wifi password. Just espresso and a conversation with the owner.
