Ossington

Ossington Ave: Still the Best Bar Strip in the City

Bellwoods Brewery. Bar Bowie. The new Bambu upstairs. Civil Liberties. The Drake Commissariat. It's not even close. Start here, always.

Bathurst & College

The Corner That Defines Toronto's Condo vs. Culture War

Sneaky Dee's has been at Bathurst & College since 1990. A 203-unit tower wants that corner. This intersection is ground zero for the future of Toronto's identity.

Kensington Market

Kensington Keeps Losing Icons — But It's Still Magic

Uncle Pete's is gone. The vintage shops rotate. But Seven Lives, Moonbean, and the Sunday pedestrian market keep this neighbourhood essential.

Dundas West

Dundas West Patio Season Is Elite — Here's Your Guide

CaféTO just launched. Paradise Grapevine, Reyna on King, PrettyUgly. The strip between Ossington and Dufferin is the patio capital of Toronto.

The Beaches

The Beaches in July: Jazz Fest, Boardwalk, and the Best Free Festival

2km of Queen Street East closed for the Beaches Jazz Fest. Plus the boardwalk, ice cream, and the kind of summer energy the west end pretends it invented.

Riverside

Riverside Eats & Beats — Why the East End Keeps Getting Better

Queen East from DVP to De Grassi. June 6. Live music, local food, actual neighbourhood spirit. The 12th year of a festival that earned its reputation.
