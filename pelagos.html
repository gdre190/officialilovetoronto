What Is Pelagos?

Pelagos (formerly Toronto Yacht Week) is an annual floating festival on Lake Ontario, against the backdrop of the Toronto skyline. It's the biggest boat party in the city — over 170 yachts tied up together, 3,000 people, live DJs, and a fully licensed onboard bar.

Whether you own a boat, charter one, or grab a ticket on a Pelagos vessel, the experience is the same: sundeck, music, skyline, people, drinks, summer. It's the event that kicks off peak summer in Toronto.

What's Included

Official yacht tie-up experience — your vessel (or your ticket) joins the main flotilla in the harbour. Live DJ all cruise long. Fully licensed onboard bar with drinks available throughout. Panoramic Toronto skyline and sunset views. Professional crew handling everything from boarding to return.

Who Goes

Birthdays, bachelorettes, groups of friends, couples. The crowd skews 25–40, well-dressed, high energy. If you've ever wanted to be on a yacht in Toronto without owning one, this is the entry point.

Good to Know

All ticket sales are final and non-refundable. In case of weather, the event may be rescheduled — tickets are honoured on the new date. Access to the cove is first-come, first-served. Dress code: summer party. Think yacht, not yacht club.

Follow @pelagosyachtweek on Instagram for lineup announcements and after-party details.
